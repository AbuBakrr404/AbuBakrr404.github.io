01. about

A little about me.

I'm Abu Bakr — a developer drawn to the messy intersection of AI, automation, and real-world workflows. Most of what I build starts with a simple question: what does someone keep doing by hand that a well-placed bit of code could just… handle?

Recent work has ranged from a Claude-powered tool that turns CV processing from a 15-minute chore into a 30-second one, to RAG-based chat over PDFs, to small but pleasing n8n workflows that quietly run in the background and make someone's morning a bit easier.

I care about tools people actually use — that means thinking about the human at the other end as much as the model in the middle.
